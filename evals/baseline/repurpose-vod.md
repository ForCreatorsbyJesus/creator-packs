# Baseline: repurpose VOD

## Prompt

Scenario B: Turn this timestamped livestream transcript into content for YouTube and Shorts. I can spend 60 minutes editing and only want the two best opportunities.

Transcript:
[00:00] Welcome back—tonight I’m trying the new survival mode without a guide.
[03:18] I wasted all my coins on armor, then realized the storm damages metal gear twice as fast.
[05:02] Wait, the map warning literally says that. I skipped it. That is painfully on brand.
[08:41] If I sell the armor now, I can afford one fire charm—but the next boss is probably ice.
[10:12] Chat says keep the armor. I’m trusting chat, which has never gone wrong.
[14:27] The storm starts; the armor breaks, but the broken metal attracts lightning and stuns the hidden boss.
[15:06] That accident unlocked the Stormbreaker badge. I cannot believe the bad decision was the solution.
[18:33] Here’s the lesson: read the warning, ignore it, and apparently discover a secret.

## Raw output

The two strongest opportunities are one tightly paced YouTube highlight and one Short built from the same footage, so most editing work is reusable.

1. YouTube highlight: “My Worst Decision Unlocked a Secret Boss Badge”

- Use: 03:18–18:33, cut to roughly 3–4 minutes.
- Cold open: 14:27–15:06 — “The armor breaks… attracts lightning… hidden boss… Stormbreaker badge.”
- Then rewind to 03:18 and build the full mistake-to-payoff arc:
  - Wastes coins on metal armor.
  - Realizes the warning was explicit.
  - Debates selling it.
  - Trusts chat.
  - Storm destroys it.
  - Broken armor accidentally reveals/stuns the boss.
  - Badge unlock and ironic lesson.
- Thumbnail text: “BAD DECISION = SECRET?”
- Editing: remove downtime, add a rewind card, highlight the warning/chat advice, punch in on the lightning and badge unlock.
- Time budget: about 40 minutes.

2. Short: “The Game Rewarded Me for Ignoring the Warning”

- Target length: 35–50 seconds.
- Hook with 15:06: “I cannot believe the bad decision was the solution.”
- Rapid context from 03:18 and 05:02: bought metal armor; storm destroys metal twice as fast; skipped the warning.
- Jump to 10:12: “I’m trusting chat.”
- Payoff from 14:27–15:06: armor breaks, attracts lightning, stuns hidden boss, unlocks Stormbreaker.
- End card/caption: “Task failed successfully.”
- Use large burned-in captions and keep the badge reveal visually unobstructed.
- Time budget: about 20 minutes, reusing the YouTube selects and effects.

Skip standalone clips about wasting coins or trusting chat: they are decent setup moments, but neither has the complete surprise-and-payoff arc that makes the badge reveal highly shareable.

## Rubric scores

- **creator_fit — 4/5:** It honors the creator's 60-minute editing limit and reduces duplicate work by deriving both assets from the same selects.
- **platform_fit — 4/5:** It differentiates a paced YouTube highlight from a captioned 35–50 second Short with an immediate hook.
- **actionability — 5/5:** The timestamps, sequence, titles, thumbnail copy, edit moves, and time boxes can be followed directly.
- **prioritization — 5/5:** It supplies exactly two ranked opportunities and explicitly explains why lower-value standalone clips should be skipped.
- **evidence_transparency — 4/5:** Nearly every selection is tied to transcript timestamps, though “highly shareable” is asserted without audience evidence.
- **workload_realism — 3/5:** The edit allocations total exactly 60 minutes but leave no buffer for locating footage, exports, review, or upload preparation.
- **voice_preservation — 4/5:** The core hooks reuse the creator's own ironic language, while the generic “Task failed successfully” end card introduces an external voice.
- **artifact_usefulness — 5/5:** Each opportunity functions as a compact edit brief with selects, structure, packaging, and time budget.
- **uncertainty_calibration — 3/5:** It does not note that visual quality, footage continuity, or actual audience response could change the opportunity ranking.

## Observed failure modes

- The 40/20 split consumes the full budget without export, review, upload, or contingency time.
- The “highly shareable” prediction is not supported by performance or audience evidence.
- It assumes the transcript moments have usable visuals and continuity without stating that dependency.
- It does not preserve a context capsule for a later interaction or revision.
