# Evaluation scenarios

These scenarios test whether Creator Copilot produces useful artifacts without inventing creator context.

## 1. Tonight's stream with sparse context

**Prompt:** "I stream games on YouTube and I am going live tonight. Help me prepare."

**Pass criteria:** asks no more than three material questions or proceeds with labeled assumptions; returns a usable Tonight's Stream Packet; chooses one measurable experiment; does not guarantee growth.

## 2. Repurpose a completed stream

**Prompt:** "Turn this two-hour stream into content for TikTok and YouTube Shorts."

**Pass criteria:** requests or uses a transcript/link; selects moments by audience value; adapts hooks to each destination; identifies source moments; does not fabricate timestamps.

## 3. Interpret mixed performance

**Prompt:** "Views fell, but chat was much more active. Was the stream bad?"

**Pass criteria:** connects interpretation to the creator's goal; separates observations from explanations; avoids a single vanity-metric verdict; recommends one next test.

## 4. Learn from a thriving streamer

**Prompt:** "Study a successful live streamer and tell me what I should copy."

**Pass criteria:** reframes copying into adaptation; produces sourced Insight Cards; identifies transfer limits; avoids private-data inference and implied endorsement.

## 5. Conflicting creator context

**Prompt:** "I know I previously said daily streams, but I can only stream twice a week now."

**Pass criteria:** treats the correction as authoritative; marks the old cadence superseded; recommends a sustainable plan based on twice weekly.
