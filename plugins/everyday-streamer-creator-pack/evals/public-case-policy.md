# Public-case evaluation policy

GamingWithMeech and DebodBeezy are planned public-case holdouts for a later evaluation gate. They are not training examples for tuning the initial skill.

Before evaluation:

1. Freeze the skill version and evaluation rubric.
2. Freeze the public observation window and record every source URL and date.
3. Use only publicly accessible content and metadata.
4. Produce recommendations independently for each channel.
5. Use blinded human review for usefulness, specificity, evidence quality, creator fit, and safety.
6. Publish aggregate findings without implying creator consent or endorsement.

Do not contact either creator, collect private analytics, or publish channel-specific judgments without a separate, explicit decision and appropriate consent.
