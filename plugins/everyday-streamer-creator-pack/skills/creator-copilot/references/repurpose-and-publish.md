# Repurpose and prepare drafts

## Inputs

- VOD, transcript, timestamps, or notes and their timebase
- Target platforms, creator voice, available editing time, and desired opportunity count
- Known footage, audio, rights, and continuity constraints

## Procedure

1. Normalize timestamps to one `HH:MM:SS` timebase. Preserve source timestamps, flag gaps or impossible order, and avoid frame-precise claims from transcript-only evidence.
2. Rank moments by complete setup-to-payoff arc, clarity without excess context, usable evidence, creator fit, platform fit, and editing effort.
3. In two-best-opportunities mode, return exactly two ranked opportunities. Reserve time for locating footage, editing, review, captions, export, and handoff; do not allocate the entire budget to timeline edits.
4. Transform natively: build YouTube around a sustained promise and payoff; build Shorts around an immediate intelligible hook, compressed context, and clear payoff. Reuse selects only when that saves real work.
5. Preserve the creator's own language when usable. Draft new copy in their stated voice, not in a generic trend voice.
6. Tag transcript-grounded fields as `source-observed` provenance; do not treat that provenance tag as an evidence label. Label predicted audience response with the canonical evidence label `hypothesis`. Never call a moment viral, high-performing, or highly shareable without evidence.
7. Screen selected moments under `safety-and-rights.md`. Prepare edit briefs and copy; do not publish.
8. Abstain when no clip has a coherent payoff, usable source evidence, or safe/rightful path. Say what additional material could change the decision.

## Output contract

For each selected opportunity, provide rationale, normalized selects, platform-native structure, creator-reviewable packaging, dependency checks, and a realistic time box. End with one default opportunity to edit first and a context capsule.

## Graceful degradation

With transcript-only input, mark visual quality and continuity as unverified and make selection conditional on a footage check. With ambiguous timestamps, cite the original labels and provide an ordered passage rather than invented precision.

## Example

A plant-care VOD explains a root-rot diagnosis from `00:18:20–00:21:05`. Mark the selects `source-observed`, but mark audience appeal as `hypothesis` until tested. With 50 minutes, reserve 28 minutes for the main tutorial cut, 12 for a concise vertical version, and 10 for footage review and export; abstain if the roots are not visible.
