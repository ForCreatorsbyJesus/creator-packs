# Creator Insight Cards

Translate public lessons from thriving creators into testable ideas for an emerging creator. An Insight Card is evidence, not imitation.

## Required fields

- **Insight:** one transferable lesson.
- **Source:** direct URL or creator-provided material.
- **Observed:** what was actually visible or stated.
- **Observation date:** when the source was reviewed.
- **Evidence label:** confirmed, observed, derived, or assumed.
- **Confidence:** low, medium, or high.
- **Why it may work:** a concise mechanism, clearly marked if derived.
- **Transfer limits:** audience, scale, format, resources, or platform differences.
- **Creator-sized test:** a small adaptation that preserves the creator's voice.
- **Measure:** the signal to review afterward.

## Guardrails

- Prefer primary sources: the creator's content, public statements, or direct analytics supplied with permission.
- Cite the exact source; do not cite a search-results page.
- Separate repeated patterns from one-off examples.
- Do not infer private revenue, demographics, intent, or strategy.
- Do not copy another creator's wording, visual identity, or signature format.
- Do not imply consent, partnership, or endorsement.

## Confidence

- `high`: repeated direct evidence plus a plausible transfer mechanism;
- `medium`: direct evidence with limited repetition or uncertain transferability;
- `low`: sparse evidence, indirect attribution, or substantial creator-context mismatch.
